About TEMO Spring Water

Building a trusted, long-term Pakistani water brand — one bottle at a time.

TEMO Spring Water was founded with a simple focus: deliver clean, dependable drinking water to the homes, offices and businesses of Islamabad and Rawalpindi — and do it consistently, every single time.

Our mission

To make pure, reliable drinking water accessible to every home, office and business we serve — through honest quality and dependable delivery.

Our vision

To grow into one of Pakistan's most trusted bottled-water brands, known for quality, consistency and genuine customer care.

Commitment

Our commitment to quality

Quality is not a single step — it runs through sourcing, treatment, bottling and delivery. We hold every stage to the same standard, because trust is earned one delivery at a time.

Who we serve

Our customers

From families stocking their kitchen to hotels, hospitals, schools and offices managing daily supply — TEMO serves customers who simply expect their water to be clean, consistent and there when they need it.

Looking ahead

Our future

We're building TEMO for the long term — expanding our delivery network across Islamabad and Rawalpindi, growing our dealer partnerships nationwide, and continuing to invest in the quality and reliability our customers count on.
