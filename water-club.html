More rewards such as cartons, vouchers or member offers can be added later from the admin side without changing the whole program.

Fair-use rules

No points on bottle security deposits, cargo/delivery charges or other non-water charges.
Cancelled, failed or refunded orders do not earn points; already-issued points are reversed where applicable.
Each new customer can trigger only one referral reward, linked to a verified unique customer record.
Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred between customers during the pilot.
Pilot points expire 12 months after they are earned unless TEMO extends them.

Admin & B2B controls

Every manual points adjustment should be stored in the audit log with admin, reason and timestamp.
Business/B2B accounts can have standard rewards enabled, disabled or replaced by contract-specific benefits.
Referral abuse checks should compare phone number, customer record and completed first delivery before awarding points.
Redemption should create a zero-price reward order or approved discount record so stock and reporting remain accurate.
Reward values remain admin-configurable before public launch.

FAQ

Before the full system goes live

Is Water Club already a paid subscription?

No. This prototype collects customer interest only. Final billing, subscription rules and payment integration will be added after the commercial model is approved.

Will reward points connect to the ERP?

That is the planned later phase. The website should first prove customer demand; then points, deliveries, referrals and bottle tracking can connect to the existing ERP/customer app architecture.

Can businesses join?

Yes. Offices, retailers, restaurants, hotels, institutions and events can request a B2B quotation and scheduled supply.
