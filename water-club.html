FAQ

Before the full system goes live

Is Water Club already a paid subscription?

No. This prototype collects customer interest only. Final billing, subscription rules and payment integration will be added after the commercial model is approved.

Will reward points connect to the ERP?

That is the planned later phase. The website should first prove customer demand; then points, deliveries, referrals and bottle tracking can connect to the existing ERP/customer app architecture.

Can businesses join?

Yes. Offices, retailers, restaurants, hotels, institutions and events can request a B2B quotation and scheduled supply.
